What is a Bounty?
A reward issued by the World Government for the capture or death of a criminal. Amounts reflect a pirate's perceived threat level shaped by power, infamy, and the chaos they've caused.

Fruit Taxonomy

02

Devil Fruit Type Distribution

What are Devil Fruits?
Mysterious fruits granting supernatural abilities at the cost of swimming. Paramecia alter the body or environment. Logia grant elemental control. Zoan allow animal transformation, including Zoan Antique (ancient/prehistoric creatures) and Zoan Mythique (mythological beings with vastly amplified power).

Haki Type Overlap & Bounty

What is Haki?
A universal power in three forms: Observation (sensing intent), Armament (invisible armor), and Conqueror's (overwhelming weaker wills, a mark of true greatness). Multi-type users consistently hold the highest bounties.
